FAQ

Why focus on lead quality?

If the ad algorithm optimizes for form fills but sales only closes 1 in 10, you are paying for junk. Scoring leads and feeding offline conversions back to Meta shifts optimization toward qualified leads.

Can you fix broken Meta tracking?

Yes — I audit Meta pixel / CAPI setups, fix deduplication and event matching quality, and implement server-side CAPI when needed.
