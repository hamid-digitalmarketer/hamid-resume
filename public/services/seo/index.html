FAQ

What does a technical SEO audit include?

A typical audit covers crawlability, indexation, canonical signals, structured data, page speed (Core Web Vitals), internal linking and site architecture, delivered as a prioritized action plan.

How is GEO different from traditional SEO?

GEO optimizes how AI answer engines extract and cite your information — using entity clarity, structured data and direct answers — while traditional SEO focuses on search-engine rankings and traffic.
